# Sourced Pitch Deck

[`sourced-pitch-deck.pptx`](sourced-pitch-deck.pptx) — 11 slides making the case for the "Sourced" Pinterest rebrand as an initiative worth pursuing further.

## Slide-by-slide

1. **Title** — "Sourced: Reimagining Pinterest for the AI era."
2. **The Challenge** — recaps the class prompt (rebrand an existing platform), shows the option list with Pinterest highlighted as our pick.
3. **Why Pinterest** — 4 documented pain points, each sourced from real user complaints: AI slop flooding feeds, dead links, scam sellers, opaque moderation.
4. **Our Concept** — the "Sourced" positioning statement as a bold quote: "Pinterest for people burned by AI slop and dead links."
5. **Feature 1 — Customizable Quick Actions** — pain point, our fix, and why it works (precedent: macOS/Windows/iOS context-menu customization).
6. **Feature 2 — Chronological Feed Toggle** — pain point, our fix, and why it works (precedent: X/Twitter, Instagram).
7. **Feature 3 — Board-View Layout Switcher** — pain point, our fix, and why it works (precedent: Notion, Airtable).
8. **Not a Guess** — validation slide; a 4-card grid naming the real product precedent behind each feature choice, so none of the three reads as invented from thin air.
9. **Proof, Not Just Slides** — a mocked-up browser window representing the working prototype, plus a checklist of what's already built and functional.
10. **Why This Should Move Forward** — the closing argument: validated pain points, proven UX patterns, and a prototype that already exists — low risk, high signal.
11. **Thank You / Questions** — closing slide.

## Notes for whoever presents this

- The palette (Pinterest red + black/white) is deliberately the app's own brand colors, not a generic template.
- Slide 9's browser mockup is illustrative, not a screenshot — if you want to swap in an actual screenshot of [`prototype/sourced-prototype.html`](../prototype/sourced-prototype.html) before presenting, that's an easy edit.
- Built with `pptxgenjs`; content and layout were manually checked for text overflow (this environment didn't have LibreOffice available for automated visual rendering QA), so give it one look-over in PowerPoint before presenting live, just in case.
